About Us

Mobile Creches was conceptualised during the planning of the Gandhi Centenary celebrations on March 14, 1969. Initially known as Kasturba Vihar, it started as a small project at Rajghat amidst the chaos of the worksite. Meera Mahadevan led the initiative, and Devika Singh joined a few months later.
